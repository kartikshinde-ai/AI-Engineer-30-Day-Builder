# Lead Classification Rules

## Course Category

### SOC
Use SOC when the lead is interested in:
- SOC Analyst
- Security Operations
- Cybersecurity SOC

### Desktop
Use Desktop when the lead is interested in:
- Desktop Support
- IT Support
- Entry-level desktop support

### VAPT
Use VAPT when the lead is interested in:
- VAPT
- Penetration Testing
- Ethical Hacking

### Other
Use Other when the lead does not clearly match SOC, Desktop, or VAPT.

---

## Lead Priority

### Hot
A lead is Hot when:
- They want to join immediately or very soon.
- Their timeline is within 1 month.
- Their message shows clear buying or joining intent.

### Warm
A lead is Warm when:
- They are interested but need more information or time.
- Their timeline is around 2–3 months.

### Cold
A lead is Cold when:
- They are only exploring options.
- They have not decided what they want.
- Their timeline is 6 months or longer.

---

## Classification Principle

Classification should be based mainly on:
1. Course interest
2. Message
3. Timeline

The same input should normally produce the same category and priority.

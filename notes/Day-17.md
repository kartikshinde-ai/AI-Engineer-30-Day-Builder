# Day 17 - Project 3 - Define Your Social Media Assistant

## Goal

Build a system to help create useful social media posts, not spam.

---

## 1. Target Audience

The target audience for the content is:

- Students interested in AI and automation
- AI and automation builders
- Beginners learning practical AI tools and workflows

---

## 2. Content Strategy

The content will focus on useful and practical learning rather than generic motivational content.

The project uses four post types:

### 1. What I Learned

Posts about concepts, tools, techniques, and lessons learned during the AI engineering journey.

### 2. What I Built

Posts showing practical AI projects, workflows, and automation systems that were built.

### 3. What Failed

Posts explaining errors, failures, debugging experiences, and lessons learned.

### 4. Simple Explainer

Beginner-friendly explanations of AI and automation concepts.

---

## 3. Human Approval Rule

There will be no automatic publishing.

The AI can prepare a social media post draft, but a human must:

1. Review the draft
2. Check the information
3. Make necessary edits
4. Approve the content
5. Publish it manually

This keeps the final publishing decision under human control.

---

## 4. Content Tracker

Created a Google Sheets content tracker for managing planned social media posts.

The tracker contains:

- Post ID
- Post Type
- Topic
- Draft
- Status
- Approval
- Publish Date
- Notes

### Status Flow

DRAFT → REVIEW → APPROVED → PUBLISHED

### Approval Values

- PENDING
- APPROVED
- REJECTED

---

## 5. Day 17 Work Completed

- Defined the target audience
- Defined the four content types
- Defined the human approval rule
- Created the Google Sheets content tracker
- Established a content process focused on useful, non-spam content

---

## 6. Key Learning

AI should assist with preparing content, but the final publishing decision should remain with a human.

Every planned post should have a useful reason to exist and should provide practical value to the audience.
